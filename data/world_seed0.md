# Synthetic world, seed 0

Nothing here is trained on. The agent is *told* the facts below, one session at a time, then asked the questions. Names are invented so the answers cannot come from real-world knowledge.

- 72 facts across 5 sessions
- 24 two-hop questions and 12 one-hop control questions
- 24 true combined facts that nobody ever states (the ground truth)
